# Scoring

- Hits follow the assigned protector. Changing equipment preserves names and scores.
- **Court Training:** body hits award the opponent 2 points; head hits award 3. Courts share continuously repeating training/break intervals. Saved durations apply from the next training interval; scores reset at the next training interval.
- **Free Training:** each valid hit received adds 1 point to the wearer. Stopwatch or repeating training/break intervals.
- **Competition:** best-of rounds with configurable point gap and gam-jeom limit. Standard gam-jeoms award 1 point; passive gam-jeoms in the final 10 seconds award 2. Both count as one penalty.
- Tied rounds compare technical points, first scoring technique (PSS or manual, excluding gam-jeoms), then (if neither scored) non-scoring hit count and first PSS hit. A remaining tie requires a referee SUP decision.
- Breaks start automatically. Start the next round from the panel.

Hits must meet the configured threshold to score. Paused sessions do not score.

IVR cards persist across rounds. A successful appeal returns the card. An unsuccessful appeal loses it and adds one gam-jeom, awarding 1 point (2 in the final 10 seconds).

Gam-jeoms and IVR are available during paused play and breaks, never during active match time. Undo IVR decision restores the card and reverses its penalty during the same round or its break.
