# Scoring

- Hits follow the assigned protector. Changing equipment preserves names and scores.
- **Court Training:** body hits award the opponent 2 points; head hits award 3. Courts share a timer.
- **Free Training:** each valid hit received adds 1 point to the wearer. No timer.
- **Competition:** best-of rounds with configurable point gap and gam-jeom limit. Each gam-jeom awards the opponent 1 point.
- Tied rounds compare technical-kick counts, head/body points, then below-threshold hit counts. A remaining tie requires a referee SUP decision.
- Breaks start automatically. Start the next round from the panel.

Hits must meet the configured threshold to score. Paused sessions do not score.
